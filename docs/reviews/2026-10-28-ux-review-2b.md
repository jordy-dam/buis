# UX review — BUIS after phase 2b

> **Case No.:** UX-20261028-001
> **Docket:** Whole app, d-pad only: picker, Home, Live TV, player, Films, Series, detail screens, Favorieten, Zoeken, Instellingen, exit
> **Presiding:** UX Judge, Laws of UX by Jon Yablonski (lawsofux.com), with a design-polish pass in the spirit of Emil Kowalski
> **Evidence:** a scripted d-pad walk-through of 22 screens on the 1080p Android TV emulator with the mock server (2026-10-28), the code, the EPG check on the real tv, and Jordy's own observations on the TCL tv.
> **Scope:** findings only. Nothing in this review has been built.

---

## Verdict: CONDITIONAL PASS

The basics are sound. Every screen works with the d-pad only, Back always does what you expect, focus comes back where you left it after the player, and the main tasks are short: resuming a film takes one OK from Home and a favourite channel two. The problems are in what a living-room viewer sees, not in flow: Live TV doesn't say what's on, and the screen wastes space. A permanent 200dp rail, 2-line channel rows and 180dp-tall posters mean that at 10 feet you see little and scroll a lot. That matches Jordy's "cards and sections are still big". Fix the two injunctions before adding features.

---

## Sanctions — Injunctions (fix in the next round)

### Mental Model / Jakob's Law — Live TV has no usable "now on"
**Violation:** The channel list shows a programme line only where the provider's per-channel short EPG answers. On the real tv that is mostly empty, and there is no "NU" marker, progress or "straks". Every IPTV app people know (TiviMate, the provider's own app, a cable box) shows what's on in the list itself.
**Order:** Import the full XMLTV guide into Room once a day for preferred-country channels. The real check measured 67 MB, 27 s and 98% coverage of the Dutch channels that have an EPG id. Show `NU · <title>` with a 2dp progress bar on every channel row, add "Straks <time> <title>" on the focused row, and use the same source for the player overlay and the Home channel row.
**Rationale:** Choosing a channel is choosing a programme. Without that information the viewer zaps blind, which is exactly the friction live TV is meant to avoid.

### Cognitive Load / Fitts's Law — Density at 10 feet
**Violation:** (1) The rail is always 200dp wide, 21% of the screen, even while you're browsing content. (2) Channel rows are at least 64dp with names on two lines, so about 7 channels fit. (3) Poster rows are about 270dp each (180dp poster, 16dp padding top and bottom, a 28dp header and 28dp gap), so about 2 rows fit.
**Order:**
- Collapse the rail to icons (about 72dp) whenever focus is in the content, and expand it when focus returns, as on the Google TV home screen.
- Make channel rows one line, 48dp: number, 40dp logo, name, `NU · title`, progress bar. About 11 channels then fit.
- Tighten poster rows to a 104dp-wide poster, 8dp vertical padding and 16dp between sections, so about 3 rows fit.
**Rationale:** With 16k channels and 69k films, every extra visible item saves a scroll. A collapsed rail is the platform's own pattern (Jakob), so it costs no learning.

## Conditional Orders

### Serial Position Effect / Fitts's Law — Zoeken sits at position 7 of the rail and needs an extra OK
**Issue:** From Home it takes 5 Downs to reach Zoeken, then Right, then OK before you can type. Search is the fastest way into 105k items.
**Order:** Move Zoeken to the first slot under the avatar, above Home, as Google TV does. When the Zoeken tab gets focus from the rail with Right, open the field straight into edit mode.
**Deadline:** Next round.

### Law of Proximity / Jakob — Season row: Down lands on the wrong season
**Issue:** On a series detail screen, Down from "Afspelen S1 A1" lands on "Seizoen 2" (the nearest tab geometrically) instead of the selected Seizoen 1. Cause: `focusRestorer()` in `SeriesDetailScreen.kt` has no fallback.
**Order:** Give the season row `focusRestorer(selectedSeason)` so the first entry lands on the selected season.
**Deadline:** Next round (a one-line fix).

### Recognition over recall (Cognitive Load) — Home tiles don't say what they are
**Issue:** "Verder kijken" tiles show only artwork and a progress bar, with no title, episode or time left. Where the artwork is missing or lacks a title, the tile is anonymous. "Favoriete zenders" on Home shows no programme.
**Order:** Under the focused tile, show the title, `S1 A3` and "nog 1:56". Channel tiles on Home get the same `NU ·` line as Live TV.
**Deadline:** With the EPG work.

### Doherty Threshold — Every focus move on the rail loads a whole screen
**Issue:** Focusing a rail item switches the tab immediately. Walking from Home to Zoeken builds Live TV, Films, Series and Favorieten on the way (paging, short-EPG requests). On the emulator that's fine; on the 32-bit TCL it may stutter.
**Order:** Switch the tab after focus has rested about 150 ms on a rail item, or on OK/Right, whichever comes first. Measure on the tv before and after.
**Deadline:** Next round, after measuring.

### Law of Prägnanz — Duplicate headings
**Issue:** On Films the page title "Films" is followed by a first row also called "Films" (the provider's category name). The same happens on Series.
**Order:** Drop the page title on Films and Series. The rail already says where you are, and the first row moves up by about 40dp.
**Deadline:** With the density work.

## Advisory Notes

- **Information architecture (Jordy's question about services and tabs):** Keep Films and Series as separate tabs (Jakob: Netflix and Google TV split them that way). Don't add genre tabs: the provider only gives genres per title (`get_vod_info`), not per category, so a "Documentaires" tab would be guesswork or 69k requests (Tesler: the system can't absorb that complexity cheaply). What does fit is putting the service categories (Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+ and so on) at the top of Films and Series as the first rows, because that is how the provider already groups them. **Open measurement first:** list the real Dutch VOD category names on the tv (names only, not stored in the repo) to see how many are services, how many are genres and how many are junk. Decide the order from that.
- **Hick's Law / Choice Overload:** Live TV with "alleen voorkeurslanden" is fine for NL. If the real NL category list is still over about 15 entries, hiding and reordering categories (already in the backlog) becomes urgent.
- **Jakob's Law:** The PIN dialog has no visible "Annuleren". Back works and that's normal on tv, so no order is issued.
- **Von Restorff:** The "Toevoegen" tile in the picker has the same size as the profiles. That's fine for now; dim it slightly if the household grows.

## Mitigating Factors — Commendations

- **Peak-End Rule:** Back from the player lands on the same channel with focus intact, and the exit dialog defaults to "Blijven". Both the end of a session and an accidental double Back end well.
- **Goal-Gradient / Zeigarnik:** "Verder kijken" first on Home with a progress bar, and "Hervatten vanaf 1:02" pre-focused on the detail screen: unfinished things are one OK away.
- **Flow:** The player overlay (number, name, time slot, progress, "Straks", "▲▼ andere zender") gives exactly enough and gets out of the way.
- **Jakob's Law:** "Wie kijkt er?" with focus on the last viewer, avatars, and switching in two OK presses works like Netflix and Plex.
- **Law of Common Region:** Instellingen split into "Voor <naam>" and "Voor iedereen" makes clear what a change affects.
- **Tesler's Law:** Names cleaned at sync, preferred countries first and the adult filter applied by the system: complexity is absorbed, not pushed onto the viewer.

## Design polish (Emil Kowalski lens)

There's little web-style animation to judge here; Compose for TV supplies focus scale and glow. The tv-relevant lessons: frequent actions must not animate or do work, unseen consistency compounds, and speed is perceived.

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focusing a rail item builds that tab at once | Switch after about 150 ms of rest, or on OK/Right | The rail is used tens of times a session; every focus step should cost nothing |
| Rail always 200dp with labels | 72dp icons while browsing content, labels on return | The content is the product; the navigation should step back once you've chosen |
| Channel row 64dp+, two-line name, no "now" | One line, 48dp: number · logo · name · `NU · title` + 2dp progress | Density plus the one fact that matters for choosing |
| Section gap 28dp, row padding 16dp, header-to-row gap ~16dp | One vertical rhythm: 16dp between sections, 8dp row padding | Inconsistent spacing reads as "big and loose" even when each value seems fine |
| Focus scale 1.1 on posters and 1.03 on rows and list items | Keep both, but use the same glow on both | Similar elements should behave alike (Law of Similarity); the glow now differs by component |
| Poster without artwork shows the title centred in grey | Title bottom-left, white, 2 lines, on a subtle gradient | A fallback should look intentional, not broken |
| Page title and first row both "Films" | No page title on Films and Series | Removing a redundant line is free space |
